关于这个应用About

从「记录 → 沉淀 → 显化 → 回顾」走一圈。顶部三个数告诉你连续了多少天、累计多少条、覆盖了哪些领域。断签了也不清零，只把「最长 41 天」摆在那里当参照。Record, settle, surface, review. Three numbers up top: current streak, total entries, and the fields you've covered. Miss a few days and nothing resets — it just shows your longest run, 41 days, as a reference.

主要功能Highlights

  • 一句话快捷记录One-line quick capture
  • 连续天数 / 累计条数 / 覆盖领域Streak / total / fields covered
  • 断签不清零No reset when you miss a day
  • 沉淀与回顾视图Settle and review views
