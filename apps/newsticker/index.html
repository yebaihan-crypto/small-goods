关于这个插件About

不想专门打开阅读软件的时候，就让它挂在桌面下沿。图片往滚动条上一拖，Vision 当场认出文字、追加进当天的播报列表；启动时它自己去「文稿 / 微信记录截图」里按当年第几天挑十张，十张轮完再来一轮，一个月不重样。滚动条没有边框，按住背景就能挪到屏幕任意位置；右键能暂停、隐藏内容、退出。控制面板里调滚动速度（px/s）、看识别进度、点开任一条改文字、删掉不想要的。识别只在本机跑，图片不出门。For the days you cannot be bothered to open a read-later app, park this along the bottom of the desktop. Drop an image on the ticker and Vision reads it on the spot, appending the text to today's list; at launch it picks ten screenshots from Documents/微信记录截图 by the day of the year, cycling through and starting over — a month before anything repeats. The bar is borderless: hold the background and drag it anywhere. Right-click to pause, hide the content or quit. The panel adjusts scroll speed in px/s, shows OCR progress, and lets you edit or delete any single item. Recognition runs on device; the images never leave.

  • 拖图片即识别，走系统 Vision OCRDrop-in OCR via the system Vision framework
  • 按当年第几天每天轮播 10 张Ten screenshots a day, chosen by day of year
  • 无边框悬浮，按住背景随意拖Borderless overlay, draggable from the background
  • 右键暂停 / 隐藏内容 / 退出Right-click to pause, hide or quit
  • 面板可调滚动速度、改文字、删条目Panel for scroll speed, text edits and deletions
  • package.sh 一键出 .app、图标与 zippackage.sh builds the .app, icon and zip in one go

一开始是为了把「微信记录截图」翻出来重读，后来发现拿来滚新闻、滚笔记、滚知识卡片都顺手 —— 只要能截成图，就能被它念一遍。源码在 GitHub 上，Swift Package 加一份打包脚本，自己 clone 下来就能跑。It started as a way to re-read a folder of WeChat screenshots, and turned out to work just as well for news, notes or knowledge cards — if it can be screenshotted, this can read it back to you. The source is on GitHub: a Swift Package plus one packaging script, clone and run.
