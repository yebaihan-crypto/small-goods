💡 关于 Pongie (乒球人) 💡 About Pongie

  • 本地安全： 采用 Apple 原生 SwiftData 本地加密存储，无服务器上传，隐私极度安全。 Local Security: Built with Apple's native SwiftData framework. No server uploads; complete offline privacy.
  • 物理情绪球池： 基于 SpriteKit 物理引擎，每次打卡生成动态乒乓球沉降碰撞。 Physics Mood Pool: Powered by SpriteKit physics. Every match creates hand-drawn balls settling with gravity and haptics.
  • 装备损耗监测： 精确追踪底板与正反手胶皮使用时长，及时提醒保养与更新。 Gear Lifespan Tracker: Accurately monitor blade and rubber wear hours with proactive maintenance reminders.
