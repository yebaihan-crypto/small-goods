关于这个应用About

拒绝冰冷单调的数字表格。「乒球人」把每一次练习、战术碰撞与对局大胜，化为沉降在物理球池里的一颗颗手绘乒乓球。胶皮什么时候该换、这个月的胜率怎么走，都能一眼看到。No cold spreadsheets. Pongie turns every practice session and every hard-won match into hand-drawn ping pong balls settling into a living physics pool — built on SpriteKit with real gravity, collision and haptics.
