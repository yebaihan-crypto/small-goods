🏓 Pongie (乒球人)

隐私政策 (Privacy Policy) | 生效日期：2026年8月17日

隐私承诺要点： “乒球人”(Pongie) 是一款注重个人隐私保护的轻量化 App。我们极度重视您的数据隐私，所有打卡记录、装备日志与分析记录均纯本地存储于您的设备中（SwiftData），我们绝不上传或售卖您的任何个人信息。

1. 我们收集的信息

乒球人遵循“最小必要”原则：

  • 本地数据： 包括您输入的乒乓球打卡记录、比赛胜负分级、底板与胶皮型号及使用时长。这些数据仅保存在您的 iPhone 本地或您个人的 iCloud 私有存储中。
  • 设备信息： 我们不收集设备的 IMEI、MAC 地址、地理位置等敏感信息。
  • 无第三方追踪： App 内未集成任何广告追踪 SDK 或第三方数据采集分析平台。

2. 数据存储与安全

您的所有数据均使用 Apple 原生 SwiftData 框架加密存储于 iOS 本地沙盒中。如果您开启了设备的 iCloud 备份，数据将随您的 iOS 系统自动备份至您的个人 Apple ID 账号下，我们无权访问您的 iCloud 空间。

3. 权限说明

  • 通知权限（可选）： 仅用于装备保养磨损到期提醒与每日打卡提醒。您随时可以在系统设置中关闭此权限。
  • 网络权限： App 完全支持离线运行，不需要蜂窝网络或 Wi-Fi 传输数据。

4. 儿童隐私保护

乒球人不会显式收集或诱导收集 13 岁以下儿童的任何个人信息。

5. 隐私政策的更新

如果我们的隐私政策有所变更，我们将在此页面及时更新版本。建议您定期查看本政策。

6. 联系我们

如果您对本隐私政策有任何疑问、建议或意见，欢迎通过以下方式与我们联系：

  • 电子邮箱： support@pongie-app.com / dev@pongie.app
  • 技术支持页面： 乒球人 Support

🏓 Pongie

Privacy Policy | Effective Date: August 17, 2026

Key Privacy Commitment: Pongie is a privacy-first, lightweight iOS application. We deeply respect and safeguard your personal data. All training logs, equipment records, and tactical analytics are stored strictly locally on your device via Apple SwiftData. We never collect, upload, track, or sell your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

Pongie adheres strictly to the principle of data minimization:

  • Local Application Data: Includes your match logs, scores, opponent notes, blade and rubber models, and play hours. This data resides solely within your device's local iOS app sandbox and your private personal iCloud container.
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not collect device IMEI, MAC address, GPS location, IDFA, or any persistent hardware identifiers.
  • Zero Third-Party Trackers: No advertising SDKs, tracking frameworks, or third-party behavioral analytics tools are embedded in Pongie.

2. Data Storage & Security

Your records are encrypted and stored locally using Apple's official SwiftData framework. If you have iCloud backup or CloudKit synchronization enabled on your iOS device, your data is synced exclusively under your personal Apple ID account. We have no access to your iCloud credentials or private storage.

3. Device Permissions

  • Notifications (Optional): Used exclusively for local rubber wear alerts and daily practice reminders. You can enable or disable this permission anytime in iOS Settings.
  • Network Access: Pongie operates completely offline. No internet connection or cellular data is required to access any core features.

4. Children's Privacy

Pongie does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions).

5. Updates to This Policy

We may periodically update our Privacy Policy. Any updates will be posted directly to this web page with a revised effective date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, feel free to reach out to our team:

  • Email: support@pongie-app.com / dev@pongie.app
  • Support Center: Pongie Support
