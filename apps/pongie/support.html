Q1: 物理情绪球池中的球体大小代表什么？ Q1: What do the different ball sizes in the physics mood pool mean?

答：大号球代表“🏆大胜比赛”，中号球代表“🏸常规对局/对练”，小号球代表“💦短时间高强度特训”。球体会根据 SpriteKit 物理引擎在球池中发生碰撞与沉降，带给您极富情趣的涂鸦打卡体验。 Answer: Large balls represent "🏆 Major Victories", medium balls represent "🏸 Regular Matches / Rallies", and small balls represent "💦 Quick Intensive Drills". Balls interact dynamically via SpriteKit physics, creating a playful hand-drawn check-in experience.

Q2: 装备日志（底板与胶皮）磨损提醒是如何计算的？ Q2: How are blade and rubber wear reminders calculated?

答：乒球人根据您每次记录的使用小时数自动累计，结合胶皮类型（如正手反胶、粘性胶皮、外套等）的平均使用寿命推荐提醒阈值。当磨损度过高时，软件会自动提示您检查胶皮粘性或考虑更换。 Answer: Pongie tracks your accumulated hours of play against recommended lifespan baselines for different rubber types (e.g. tacky inverted, European tension, short pips). When wear exceeds optimal thresholds, the app gently suggests inspecting surface tack or planning a replacement.

Q3: 如何添加 iOS 桌面小组件 (Widget)？ Q3: How do I add iOS home screen widgets?

答：长按 iOS 桌面任意空白处 -> 点击左上角“+”加号 -> 搜索“Pongie”或“乒球人” -> 选择适合您桌面的小组件尺寸（中号或小号）并添加即可。 Answer: Long-press any blank area on your Home Screen -> Tap the "+" icon in the top corner -> Search for "Pongie" -> Choose your preferred widget size and tap Add Widget.

Q4: 我的打卡记录如果换手机了怎么办？ Q4: What happens to my data if I switch to a new phone?

答：乒球人使用系统内置的 SwiftData 存储，只要您的系统开启了 iCloud 自动同步与备份，在更换新 iPhone 时登录同一个 Apple ID 即可自动恢复所有数据。 Answer: Pongie utilizes native iOS SwiftData. As long as iCloud sync and backup are enabled, logging into your Apple ID on a new iPhone will restore all your logs automatically.
