常见问题 (FAQ)

Q1: 物理情绪球池中的球体大小代表什么？

答：大号球代表“🏆大胜比赛”，中号球代表“🏸常规对局/对练”，小号球代表“💦短时间高强度特训”。球体会根据 SpriteKit 物理引擎在球池中发生碰撞与沉降，带给您极富情趣的涂鸦打卡体验。

Q2: 装备日志（底板与胶皮）磨损提醒是如何计算的？

答：乒球人根据您每次记录的使用小时数自动累计，结合胶皮类型（如正手反胶、粘性胶皮、外套等）的平均使用寿命推荐提醒阈值。当磨损度过高时，软件会自动提示您检查胶皮粘性或考虑更换。

Q3: 如何添加 iOS 桌面小组件 (Widget)？

答：长按 iOS 桌面任意空白处 -> 点击左上角“+”加号 -> 搜索“Pongie”或“乒球人” -> 选择适合您桌面的小组件尺寸（中号或小号）并添加即可。

Q4: 我的打卡记录如果换手机了怎么办？

答：乒球人使用系统内置的 SwiftData 存储，只要您的系统开启了 iCloud 自动同步与备份，在更换新 iPhone 时登录同一个 Apple ID 即可自动恢复所有数据。

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: What do the different ball sizes in the Physics Mood Pool represent?

Answer: Ball sizes dynamically represent match intensity — 🏆 Large Balls indicate "Decisive Victories / Major Matches", 🏸 Medium Balls indicate "Regular Matches & Friendly Rallies", and 💦 Small Balls indicate "Intense Drills & Practice Routines". Driven by Apple's SpriteKit physics engine, balls clash and settle realistically in the pool.

Q2: How does the Gear Log calculate rubber and blade wear?

Answer: Pongie automatically accumulates active hours logged from your match sessions. Combined with recommended durability thresholds for inverted, tacky, and tensor rubbers, it calculates remaining life percentages. When wear approaches critical levels, Pongie alerts you to inspect tackiness or plan a replacement.

Q3: How do I add Pongie iOS Home Screen Widgets?

Answer: Long press any empty space on your iOS Home Screen until the apps jiggle -> Tap the "+" icon in the top left corner -> Search for "Pongie" -> Choose your preferred widget size (Small or Medium) and tap "Add Widget".

Q4: How do I restore my match history when switching to a new iPhone?

Answer: Pongie utilizes Apple's native SwiftData framework. As long as your iOS device has iCloud Backup enabled under your Apple ID, your data automatically restores when you set up your new iPhone.
