一、概述与隐私承诺

欢迎使用《家常菜谱与美食点餐记录》（以下简称“本应用”）。我们深知个人隐私对您的重要性，并始终严格遵守适用于 App Store 及相关法律法规的隐私保护要求。

本隐私政策旨在清晰透明地向您说明本应用如何收集、使用、存储及保护您的个人信息。

二、我们收集的信息及用途

本应用的核心设计理念是“本地优先”与“数据隐私安全”。我们仅在提供 App 功能所必需的前提下向您申请相关权限：

1. 相册与相机权限 (Camera & Photo Library)

当您为菜谱添加或拍摄菜品图片时，本应用会申请调用您的摄像头或读取相册。所获取的照片仅用于显示在您的本地菜谱卡片中，绝不会上传至任何第三方商业服务器。

2. 麦克风与语音识别权限 (Microphone & Speech Recognition)

当您使用“语音录入菜谱/听写烹饪步骤”功能时，本应用会申请麦克风与系统语音识别权限。语音数据仅用于实时转写为文本，转写完成后即刻在本地展示，绝不存储或滥用您的音频原始记录。

3. iCloud 存储数据 (iCloud Private Database)

若您在设备上开启了 iCloud 同步功能，您的菜谱、点餐历史与偏好设置将上传至您个人的 Apple iCloud 私有数据库（CloudKit）。该数据归您个人所有，且受 Apple 加密保护，开发者及任何第三方均无权访问或调阅。

三、第三方 SDK 与广告追踪

本应用不包含任何第三方广告 SDK、第三方行为追踪统计工具或任何形式的定向广告推荐。我们承诺绝不上报或兜售您的个人上网轨迹或使用数据。

四、儿童隐私保护

本应用适合所有年龄段（包括儿童）使用。我们不会故意收集任何 13 周岁以下儿童的个人身份信息。

五、隐私政策的更新与联系方式

随着功能的升级，我们可能会适时更新本隐私政策。若有重大变更，我们会在本页面公开发布。

1. Overview & Privacy Commitment

Welcome to Cookbook & Smart Meal Planner ("we", "our", or "the App"). We deeply respect your privacy and adhere strictly to App Store guidelines and applicable privacy regulations.

This Privacy Policy explains clearly and transparently how the App collects, uses, and safeguards your personal data.

2. Information We Collect & Usage

Our core philosophy is "Local-First" and "Absolute Data Privacy". Permissions are requested strictly when needed for core functionality:

1. Camera & Photo Library Access

When you capture or import photos of your dishes, the App requests camera or photo library access. Photos remain strictly within your local recipe cards and are never uploaded to any third-party commercial servers.

2. Microphone & Speech Recognition

When using the "Voice Recipe Dictation" feature, the App requests microphone and speech recognition access. Audio is transcribed to text on-device in real-time and raw audio recordings are never stored or retained.

3. iCloud Storage (CloudKit Private Database)

When you enable iCloud sync, your recipes, ordering history, and settings sync securely via Apple's official CloudKit private database. The data belongs entirely to you, is encrypted by Apple, and cannot be viewed or accessed by us or any third parties.

3. Third-Party SDKs & Advertising

The App contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, tracking analytics, or targeted ad networks. We never sell, track, or commercialize your personal data or activity.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

We may update this policy periodically to reflect feature improvements. Any substantial updates will be posted here.
