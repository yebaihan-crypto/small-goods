关于这部短片About this short

一部面向 0–6 岁幼儿的手绘动画短片。来自遥远宇宙的小使者笑笑，被派去和地球星人「建交」；他降落到一个花园，遇见蚂蚁和小小的植物，在相处中懂得了友善、分享与帮助别人。A hand-drawn short made for children aged 0–6. Xiaoxiao, a little envoy from a distant universe, is sent to make contact with the Earthlings. He lands in a garden, meets ants and tiny plants, and learns kindness, sharing and helping others along the way.

创作命题The brief

「爱德说」采取命题创作，围绕「心怀他人，培养美德」「知礼懂礼，文明礼貌」「尊重他人，树立边界」「理解规则，遵守秩序」四大品行范畴展开。本片对应「心怀他人」——关心他人、乐于分享、宽容友善。The programme sets a theme: works must address one of four character strands — caring for others, good manners, respecting others, and following rules. This short takes on “caring for others” — concern, sharing and friendliness.

关于「爱德说」About “Aide Shuo”

「爱德说」幼儿德育作品征集活动（原「正邦说」）由中国少年儿童文化艺术基金会与深圳市爱圣公益基金会联合主办，面向全社会征集适合 0–6 岁幼儿的故事、歌曲、绘本与动画片。活动设年度杰出、优异、入围等荣誉，其最高荣誉「爱德卓越作品」的公益金为 200 万元（全活动至多评出 1 部）。The “Aide Shuo” programme (formerly “Zhengbang Shuo”) is co-organised by the China Children's Art Foundation and the Shenzhen Aisheng Charity Foundation, inviting stories, songs, picture books and animated films for children aged 0–6. It grants annual outstanding, excellent and shortlisted honours; its top honour, the “Aide Excellence” award, carries a ¥2,000,000 grant (at most one work across the whole programme).
